# Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze sales, profitability, discounts, product performance, customer segments, geographical performance, and monthly business trends.

The dashboard transforms transactional business data into an interactive visual report that helps identify key sales and profit patterns and compare performance across countries, products, customer segments, and time periods.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on developing an interactive sales analytics dashboard using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business performance indicators and allows users to explore sales and profitability patterns through interactive filters and visualizations.

The analysis covers:

- Overall sales performance
- Profitability and discounts
- Product contribution to sales
- Country-wise sales performance
- Customer segment performance
- Monthly sales trends
- Monthly profit trends

The objective is to turn raw business data into meaningful visual insights that can support data-driven business analysis.

## Key Insights

- Total sales across the analyzed data amount to approximately **118.73M**, with total profit of approximately **16.89M**.
- The **United States, Canada, France, Germany, and Mexico** are the top five countries by sales, with each contributing more than 20M in sales.
- **Paseo** is the largest contributor to sales among the products shown, followed by **VTT** and **Velo**.
- The **Government** customer segment records the highest number of units sold among the segments analyzed, significantly exceeding the other segments.
- Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year, with **October recording the highest monthly sales at approximately 22M**.
- Monthly profit also varies considerably, with **October recording the highest profit at approximately 3.4M**.
- The dashboard allows users to analyze performance across **2013 and 2014** using interactive year and country filters.
- The comparison of monthly sales and profit provides a clear view of how business performance changes over time.

## Analytical Approach

The project followed a structured analytics workflow:

1. **Data Preparation**
   - Reviewed the available business data.
   - Identified relevant fields for sales, profit, discounts, products, countries, customer segments, and time.

2. **Data Transformation**
   - Prepared and transformed the data using Power Query.
   - Ensured the data was suitable for analysis and visualization.

3. **Data Modeling**
   - Structured the data model within Power BI.
   - Created the required calculations and measures using DAX.

4. **Dashboard Development**
   - Designed KPI cards and analytical visuals.
   - Added interactive filters for year and country.

5. **Analysis**
   - Compared sales and profitability across countries, products, customer segments, and months.
   - Identified major contributors and significant trends.
